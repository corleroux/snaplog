# 04 — Back-a-Buddy campaign pack

Owner marketing, separate from the hackathon submission. Back-a-Buddy is
a South African crowdfunding platform; contributions are **donations
toward a self-funded startup's launch costs — not investments, no equity,
no returns promised.** Say that plainly on the page.

1. **Snaplog: Help a Self-Funded SA Startup Turn Trade-Fair Photos Into Catalogues**
2. **Snap It. Catalogued. — Back the Launch of Snaplog**
3. **From Camera Roll to Catalogue: Fund Snaplog's First Season**

## The hook (first 3 lines of the campaign page)

> I'm building Snaplog alone, self-funded, from South Africa.
> It turns the photos trade-fair buyers already take into structured,
> searchable catalogues — and it just competed in a global Meta AI
> hackathon. Your backing gets it from demo to the first real fairs.

## Story structure (page body, in this order)

1. **The problem I kept watching.** Buyers at fabric & garment fairs meet
   dozens of vendors, photograph every label and rack, then lose days
   matching photos to notes in a spreadsheet. The knowledge from a fair
   evaporates into a camera roll.
2. **What Snaplog does.** Photograph a label or garment → AI drafts the
   record against a schema → the buyer reviews (low-confidence fields
   flagged, never guessed) → it lands in a searchable catalogue:
   Event → Vendor → Item → Variant, always traceable to the source photo.
3. **Proof it works.** This week, for the Meta × Cerebral Valley AI
   Hackathon: extraction used to run on Gemini; this week we moved it
   behind a provider interface onto Muse Spark. Garments were added as a
   schema definition with no application code changes, and rack video is
   catalogued item-by-item in the background (Modal + SAM 3.1 via the
   Meta Model API). Built with Muse Spark Open. Link the demo:
   {{VIDEO_URL}}
4. **Why crowdfunding, why now.** No investors, no salary — every rand so
   far is mine. The next step is unglamorous and specific: put Snaplog in
   real buyers' hands at real fairs and pay the running costs while the
   first customers come on board.
5. **What the money does** (owner: itemise before publishing — guidance
   categories below, owner fills amounts).
6. **What happens either way.** The product keeps being built in public;
   backers get updates at every milestone (below). If the campaign
   underfunds, contributions still go to the same listed costs in
   priority order — say so honestly.

## Goal-framing guidance (owner-supplied number recorded above — none was suggested by us)

- Build the goal bottom-up from 3–5 line items with real quotes, e.g.:
  cloud/API running costs for the first season (R___), a work phone /
  device for fair-floor testing (R___), travel + entry to the first
  target trade fairs (R___), company/admin essentials (R___).
- Prefer a goal you can fully account for over a round aspirational
  number; South African backers respond to itemised, modest, specific.
- Add a stretch line: "If we pass the goal, the extra goes to ___"
  (one named thing — e.g. the next fair's travel).
- Timeline honesty: "Funds are used over the next ___ months; updates
  monthly until spent."

## Backer acknowledgements (no equity, no returns — gratitude only)

- Early look: backers see new features before public posts (a viewing,

- First-season stories: a short "your money did this" update from the

## Update cadence (commit to this on the page)

Launch day → weekly during the hackathon-engagement window (to Nov 8) →
monthly until funds are spent, each with one photo/receipt-level fact:
what shipped, what it cost, what's next.

## FAQ starters

- *Is this an investment?* No — it's a donation to a self-funded
  founder. No shares, no returns; my accountability is public updates.
- *What if the startup doesn't make it?* The money will have been spent
  on the listed launch costs, reported as I go. The software and what it
  taught the local ecosystem don't disappear either way.
- *Why not just get a job/investors?* I am pursuing customers and
  partners in parallel (LinkedIn post, 03). Crowdfunding lets the people
  who want this to exist help directly, early.
